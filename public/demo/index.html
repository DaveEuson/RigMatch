macOS first launch

RigMatch.AI is an unsigned beta distributed outside the App Store. macOS may block the first launch, even when the download is fine.

  1. Download mac-arm64 for Apple Silicon or mac-x64 for Intel.
  2. Open the dmg and drag RigMatch.AI into Applications.
  3. First launch only: right-click or Control-click the app, choose Open, then choose Open again.
  4. If macOS still blocks it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

What stays local

RigMatch talks to Ollama on your machine. The benchmark prompts, model answers, scores, and top-pick history stay local unless you choose to share a bug report.

Need Ollama first? Install it from ollama.com, then come back and run Check Local in RigMatch.
