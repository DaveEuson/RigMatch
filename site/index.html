Picking a local model shouldn’t take a degree in graphics memory.

  1. It reads your PC

    Graphics card, memory and disk, in one click. Only models that fit get to audition.

  2. You pick the contestants

    Say what you want: chat, writing, code, pictures. Pick up to five.

  3. The show runs

    Same questions, same stage, one at a time, timed on your hardware. Every answer is kept so you can read it.

  4. Meet your Top Match

    One score, one reason, and a button to start chatting with the winner.

See what fits before you download

Every model gets a Fit grade for your computer, from A to F, before you test or download anything. It weighs whether the model fits in your graphics memory, how fast it should run and how good it is for its size. Click a grade to read the reasons.

Or ask from a terminal

Blind dates

Turn on Blind show in Advanced Mode and the contestants become Contestant 1, 2 and 3. Their names, sizes and scores stay hidden while they answer.

At the end you read their answers side by side and pick the one you’d rather use. Then the names come out, with the judge’s winner beside your pick. Your picks add up into a ranking of your own, Your taste, kept apart from the judge’s scores.

Can it draw a pelican riding a bicycle?

The General question set now includes a drawing round, borrowed from Simon Willison’s pelican benchmark. Each model answers with an SVG picture. RigMatch cleans it so nothing in it can run, then the judge scores it.

One honest number

The Match Score blends four measurements, all taken on your computer. Slide between accuracy first and speed first and the ranking updates, without running anything again.

  • Answer quality× 0.34

    Checked by rules where there is a right answer, and by a judge model where there isn’t.

  • Speed× 0.32

    Tokens a second and time to the first word, measured on your hardware.

  • Finish rate× 0.18

    Whether it finishes its answers, without errors or running out of time.

  • Fit× 0.16

    How comfortably it sits in your memory, leaving room for everything else.

How fast is your PC, really?

Speed score, under My PC in Advanced Mode, runs the same test on any computer and gives one number you can set beside someone else’s. An RTX 4070 scores about 1,000, and the same PC can vary by 10 to 15 percent from one run to the next.

Ask two models at once

In RigMatch Chat, Ask a second model too sends each message to two models and shows their answers side by side. Each keeps its own conversation, and Keep this one goes back to a normal chat with the one you preferred.

Nothing leaves your computer

Models run through Ollama and ComfyUI on your own hardware. No account, no telemetry. The exceptions are the OpenRouter judge and Ollama’s cloud models, which you’d have to choose yourself, and RigMatch shows what would leave your computer before every run.

  • No account
  • No telemetry
  • Models stay on your disk
  • Source-available on GitHub
